# K10 — Psychological Distress Guide

## Overview
The K10 is a screening tool designed to measure general psychological distress. It assesses the frequency of distress-related symptoms such as nervousness, fatigue, and hopelessness over recent weeks. The K10 is often used in population health, primary care, and mental health screening programs.

## What It Measures
- Nervousness
- Sadness
- Restlessness
- Fatigue
- Emotional strain

## Score Interpretation
- **10–15**: Low distress  
- **16–21**: Moderate distress  
- **22–29**: High distress  
- **30–50**: Very high distress  

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores suggest increased emotional distress affecting coping ability.

## Safety & Support Note
Very high distress may indicate the need for additional support. If distress feels unsafe or unbearable:
- Contact a trusted person or professional
- Seek emergency help if needed

## Important Notes
- Distress may be temporary or situational.
- High scores are not diagnoses.
