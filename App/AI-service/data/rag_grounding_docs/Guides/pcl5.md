# PCL-5 — Trauma Symptoms Guide

## Overview
The PCL-5 is a self-report questionnaire designed to assess symptoms related to post-traumatic stress. It reflects experiences associated with trauma exposure, including intrusive memories, avoidance behaviors, and heightened arousal. The scale is used to screen for trauma-related symptom patterns and monitor symptom changes over time.

## What It Measures
- Intrusive memories
- Avoidance
- Mood changes
- Hypervigilance
- Sleep problems

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores suggest trauma-related stress may be affecting well-being.

## Safety & Support Note
Trauma symptoms can be intense. If distress feels overwhelming or unsafe:
- Professional trauma-informed support is recommended
- Seek immediate help if safety is at risk

## Important Notes
- Trauma responses vary widely.
- This tool does not confirm a diagnosis.
