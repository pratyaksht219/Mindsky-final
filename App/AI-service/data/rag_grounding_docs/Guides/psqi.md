# PSQI — Sleep Quality Guide

## Overview
The PSQI is a standardized tool used to evaluate sleep quality and sleep-related difficulties. It assesses multiple aspects of sleep, including duration, disturbances, and daytime functioning, over the past month. The PSQI is commonly used in both clinical practice and sleep research.

## What It Measures
- Sleep duration
- Sleep disruptions
- Daytime fatigue
- Sleep satisfaction

## Score Interpretation
- **≤5**: Good sleep quality  
- **>5**: Poor sleep quality  

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores suggest sleep issues that may affect mood, energy, or concentration.

## Safety & Support Note
Poor sleep can worsen emotional health. If sleep problems contribute to distress or emotional difficulty:
- Support and evaluation may help
- Seek help if sleep loss affects safety

## Important Notes
- Sleep issues often relate to stress or anxiety.
- Improving sleep routines can improve well-being.
