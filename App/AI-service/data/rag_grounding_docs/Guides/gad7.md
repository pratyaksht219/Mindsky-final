# GAD-7 — Anxiety Screening Guide

## Overview
The GAD-7 is a brief screening tool used to identify symptoms of generalized anxiety. It is frequently used in clinical and community settings to assess persistent worry, nervousness, and related emotional distress over the past two weeks. The scale helps highlight anxiety symptoms that may impact daily functioning.

## What It Measures
- Excessive worry
- Restlessness
- Difficulty relaxing
- Nervousness or tension
- Irritability
- Fear of negative outcomes

## Score Interpretation
- **0–4**: Minimal anxiety  
- **5–9**: Mild anxiety  
- **10–14**: Moderate anxiety  
- **15–21**: Severe anxiety  

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores indicate anxiety symptoms may be frequent or intense enough to interfere with daily life.

## Safety & Support Note
Anxiety can sometimes feel overwhelming or lead to panic or distress. If anxiety feels unmanageable or leads to thoughts of harm:
- Consider reaching out for professional support
- Seek immediate assistance if safety feels at risk

## Important Notes
- Anxiety symptoms can be situational or ongoing.
- Results are informational, not diagnostic.
- Support and coping strategies are available.
