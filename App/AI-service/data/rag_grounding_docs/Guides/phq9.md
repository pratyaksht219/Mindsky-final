# PHQ-9 — Depression Screening Guide

## Overview
The PHQ-9 is a widely used screening questionnaire designed to assess the presence and severity of depressive symptoms in adults. It is commonly used in primary care, mental health, and research settings to identify patterns of low mood and functional impairment over the past two weeks. The PHQ-9 helps clinicians and individuals recognize symptoms that may warrant further evaluation.

## What It Measures
- Low mood or sadness
- Loss of interest or pleasure
- Fatigue or low energy
- Sleep disturbances
- Appetite changes
- Difficulty concentrating
- Feelings of worthlessness or guilt

## Score Interpretation
- **0–4**: Minimal symptoms  
- **5–9**: Mild symptoms  
- **10–14**: Moderate symptoms  
- **15–19**: Moderately severe symptoms  
- **20–27**: Severe symptoms  

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores suggest depressive symptoms may be affecting emotional well-being, motivation, and daily functioning.

## Safety & Support Note
Some individuals experiencing depressive symptoms may also experience thoughts of self-harm or feeling overwhelmed. If distress feels intense, persistent, or unsafe:
- Reach out to a trusted person
- Contact a mental health professional
- Seek immediate help from local emergency services if you feel at risk

## Important Notes
- This tool does **not** provide a medical diagnosis.
- Symptoms can change due to stress, illness, or life circumstances.
- Professional support can help with understanding and managing symptoms.
