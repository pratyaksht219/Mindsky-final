# PSS-10 — Perceived Stress Guide

## Overview
The PSS-10 measures the degree to which individuals perceive situations in their lives as stressful. Rather than focusing on specific symptoms, it evaluates feelings of unpredictability, lack of control, and overload experienced over the past month. The scale is commonly used in stress and coping research.

## What It Measures
- Sense of overwhelm
- Ability to cope
- Perceived control
- Emotional response to pressure

## Score Interpretation
- **0–13**: Low stress  
- **14–26**: Moderate stress  
- **27–40**: High stress  

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores suggest feeling overloaded or strained by life demands.

## Safety & Support Note
High stress can impact emotional health. If stress contributes to emotional distress or harmful thoughts:
- Consider reaching out for support
- Seek immediate help if safety is a concern

## Important Notes
- Stress perception varies widely.
- Stress can often be reduced with support and coping strategies.
