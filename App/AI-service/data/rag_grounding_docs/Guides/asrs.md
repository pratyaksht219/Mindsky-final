# ASRS — Adult ADHD Self-Report Guide

## Overview
The ASRS is a screening questionnaire developed to identify attention-related symptoms in adults. It focuses on difficulties with concentration, organization, and impulsivity that may affect daily functioning. The scale is widely used in both clinical and general population settings to flag attention concerns that may require further evaluation.

## What It Measures
- Difficulty focusing
- Disorganization
- Forgetfulness
- Restlessness
- Impulsivity

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores suggest attention difficulties that may affect daily functioning.

## Safety & Support Note
Attention difficulties can be frustrating and emotionally draining. If these challenges cause significant distress:
- Professional guidance may help
- Emotional support is important

## Important Notes
- This tool does not diagnose ADHD.
- Symptoms may overlap with stress or anxiety.
