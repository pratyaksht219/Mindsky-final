# LSAS — Social Anxiety Guide

## Overview
The LSAS is a screening tool used to assess social anxiety symptoms, including fear and avoidance of social or performance situations. It is commonly used in mental health settings to evaluate how social anxiety impacts daily interactions and quality of life. The scale examines both emotional discomfort and behavioral avoidance.

## What It Measures
- Fear of social judgment
- Avoidance behaviors
- Anxiety in public or performance settings

## What These Scores Mean
Higher scores indicate greater social discomfort or avoidance.

## Safety & Support Note
Social anxiety can feel isolating. If avoidance or fear causes distress or impacts well-being:
- Support and gradual coping strategies can help
- Seek professional support if needed

## Important Notes
- Social anxiety exists on a spectrum.
- Many people experience situational social discomfort.
